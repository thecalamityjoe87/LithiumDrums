Lithium Drums

A sampled acoustic drum kit by Isaac Joseph (Calamity Audio). It works two ways:

  • As a plugin (VST3, CLAP or Audio Unit) on an instrument track in your DAW.
  • As a standalone app in Applications, with no DAW needed: play it from an e-kit or MIDI keyboard.

Click Customize on the Installation Type page to choose which of these to install. All are installed by default.

The drum kits aren't in this installer. Open Lithium Drums and pick one in the kit browser; it downloads it for you.
